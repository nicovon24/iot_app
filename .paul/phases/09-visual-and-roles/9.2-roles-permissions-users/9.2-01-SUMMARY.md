## What was built
`ReaderBlockGuard` (`backend/src/common/guards/reader-block.guard.ts`), a new global `APP_GUARD`
registered third in `app.module.ts` (after `SessionAuthGuard`, `CustomerScopeGuard`). It throws
403 (`ForbiddenException`) on any `POST`/`PUT`/`PATCH`/`DELETE` request when `session.appRole ===
'READER'`. No-ops when `request.session` is unset (deferring to `SessionAuthGuard`/`@Public()`)
and never blocks GET/HEAD/OPTIONS or ADMIN/SYSADMIN sessions (`appRole` is `null` for
SYSADMIN/TENANT_ADMIN, so no separate bypass branch was needed).
